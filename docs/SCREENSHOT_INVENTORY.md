## Screenshot admission rule

A future screenshot may be presented as a verified application capture only when its inventory row records:
- The exact application repository and commit/build SHA.
- The route or screen captured and the relevant environment.
- Capture date and responsible reviewer.
- Evidence that the image came from that exact application build.

If those conditions are not met, the visual must carry the explicit visible label **“Illustrative Preview”** and must not be described as a real product screen. Never use fabricated records, balances, customer names or business KPIs to make a concept image look operational.

AC-11 is currently satisfied by the absence of screenshots: there are no unverified captured application screens in the repository. Any future addition of screenshots or raster media requires this inventory to be updated in the same change.
